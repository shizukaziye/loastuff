How this page works out its numbers

Bracelet. The grade (0–100), its letter and the damage figure are the bracelet leaderboard’s own: the bracelet-bible Worker scores every pulled bracelet on the calculator’s default character, and the letter comes from the same subrank ladder the board uses. A character with more than one bracelet across its lostark.bible loadouts is ranked on the best one, as on the board.

Bracelet rank. The character’s place on its region’s board, in the board’s own order. The DPS board ranks everyone by damage %, less support mains; the Support board ranks Bard, Paladin, Artist and Valkyrie by what one damage dealer gains. A support class shows on the board where its letter is better. Top X% is the place divided by the number on that board. The board rebuilds at most every 10 minutes; a character it has not picked up yet (a new pull, or one just updated) is scored here with the calculator’s own model, and its place is marked ≈ until the board catches up.

Astrogems. Quality, letter and grid damage are the astrogem leaderboard’s, from the astrogem calculator’s model run on the character’s gems. The rank is the place on the region’s astrogem board, sorted by total grid damage (party damage on the Support board); that board rebuilds at most every 30 minutes. A gem’s grade is the game’s: its four levels add up to 4–15 for Legendary, 16–18 for Relic and 19–20 for Ancient.

Stats. The raid loadout’s figures as lostark.bible reports them, which the bracelet calculator imports into its character panel. Update reads the bracelet record again; astrogems are pulled from the Astrogem Calculator, which needs a lostark.bible sign-in.

Where the data comes from. Public lostark.bible character pages, as their owners last synced them — it can be weeks behind the game. Raid statistics are never read. Not affiliated with Smilegate, Amazon Games or lostark.bible.
