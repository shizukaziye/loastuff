How this page works out its numbers

Bracelet. The grade (0–100), its letter and the damage figure are the bracelet leaderboard’s own: the bracelet-bible Worker scores every pulled bracelet on the calculator’s default character, and the letter comes from the same subrank ladder the board uses. A character with more than one bracelet across its lostark.bible loadouts is ranked on the best one, as on the board.

Bracelet rank. The character’s place on its region’s board, in the board’s own order. The DPS board ranks everyone by damage %, less support mains; the Support board ranks Bard, Paladin, Artist and Valkyrie by what one damage dealer gains. A support class shows on the board the Worker reads it on. Top X% is the place divided by the number on that board. The board rebuilds at most every 10 minutes; a character it has not picked up yet (a new pull, or one just updated) is scored here with the calculator’s own model, and its place is marked ≈ until the board catches up.

Astrogems. Every figure comes from the astrogem calculator’s model. Quality and the rank are the astrogem leaderboard’s: a geometric mean of the gems’ values, and the place on the region’s board by total grid damage (party damage on the Support board), which rebuilds at most every 30 minutes. The table, the average grade, the per-core figures and Total % dmg are the Grader’s, on the axis the Grader opens this character on: Support for a support class whose gems carry mostly support effects, DPS otherwise. The grid-damage split runs the model’s own grid formula once per source with the others set to zero, so the parts add up to the total. A gem’s grade is the game’s: its four levels add up to 4–15 for Legendary, 16–18 for Relic and 19–20 for Ancient.

Gold per 1% damage. The GPD chart’s own lookup (/loa-gpd/lookup.js), run here on the same two lostark.bible answers the cards above use: each system’s rung, and what the next step costs per 1% damage at the chart’s default prices and switches (no flat line, high main stat). A support is graded on the support ladders, where gold per 1% counts the buff on all three dealers. Only a step under 25M per 1% can be the pick. It loads after the cards above, so it never holds them up.

Stats. The raid loadout’s figures as lostark.bible reports them, which the bracelet calculator imports into its character panel. Update reads the bracelet record again; astrogems are pulled from the Astrogem Calculator, which needs a lostark.bible sign-in.

Where the data comes from. Public lostark.bible character pages, as their owners last synced them — it can be weeks behind the game. Raid statistics are never read. Not affiliated with Smilegate, Amazon Games or lostark.bible.
