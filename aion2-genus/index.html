When to lock

Each lock makes every later press dearer, so your standard should fall as the board fills. The table gives the lowest roll worth locking, by how many slots are already locked. Lock in whatever order the lines land.

Orange needs a Heroic roll, gold a Unique one. any means lock it at any Unique or Heroic value. A dash means leave it: no roll is worth the lock at that stage. Leave slots 2 and 8 open; they roll nothing that adds damage. These are averages over the ways a board can fill; the cards below give the exact number for a board as it stands.

How it works

The press. Analyze re-rolls every slot you have not locked. It costs 4 Soul Crystals and 1,000 Kinah for each slot it rolls, plus a charge for the locks. The charge grows fast: a press costs 36 crystals with nothing locked, 96 with four locks and 172 with five.

The odds. At Insight 10 each slot rolls Heroic 5% of the time and Unique 15%. The game then draws one line from the slot's list, each as likely as the next: ten lines in slots 1, 3, 5, 6 and 9, twelve in slots 2 and 8, and fourteen in slots 4 and 7 (twelve on the Special board). So one named line at Heroic is one press in 200 on most slots and one in 280 on slot 4.

The choice. After a press you can stop and keep the board, press again, or lock some lines and press again. The page weighs each choice with one price: the Soul Crystals you would pay for +1% damage. A line is worth a lock when its damage, at that price, beats what the lock adds to every press still to come. Your budget sets the price, and the page solves every board state exactly from there.

Damage. Each line is priced on your sheet with the Damage Math model. Pick "My Damage Math sheet" to use the sheet you saved there.

Critical Hit. Every 10 points above the boss's Critical Hit Resist is 1% crit, up to 50%. Past that a point is worth nothing, so the plan stops buying crit lines at the cap. A Front or Back Attack Critical Hit line counts only on hits from that side.

Accuracy. A hit from the front or side is parried when your Accuracy is below the boss's Block: about 6.7% of hits for each 100 you are short, up to 80%. A parried hit deals about half its damage. Hits from behind are never parried. So closing a gap of 200 from the front is worth about 6% damage, and Accuracy past the boss's Block is worth nothing. Accuracy Bonus and PvE Accuracy both count.

Half for capped stats. Critical Hit and Accuracy count at half their damage when the page chooses what to lock. Both are worth nothing once you pass the cap, and each gear upgrade moves you closer to it. The headline shows the counted figure; "Damage today" shows every line at full.

Front and back. You fight from one side. A front line counts on the share of hits you land from the front, 85% when you play the front. A back line counts on the rest. Play the back and it is 70% from behind, 30% from the front.

Crystals a week. As of late November 2026 a free routine brings about 6,500 a week: Odylium Repository runs, Substance Morph, the Festival shop and the attendance board. Each character can also buy three Hidden Cube keys a day for 50 Quna each, and a keyed cube pays 1,000 crystals. That is about 27,500 a week on one character.

What the page assumes

  • Every board is at Insight 10. Do not lock anything before then.
  • Rolls fall evenly inside a grade's range. The client does not say.
  • Unique ranges for Attack, Critical Hit and Accuracy lines are the printed minimum doubled, the pattern every printed range follows. Check them in the Auto Analysis window, which shows the minimum and maximum for each grade.
  • Lines below Unique grade count as nothing.
  • A genus Damage Boost line adds to the same sum as Damage Boost and PvE Damage Boost, and only against bosses of that genus. Nobody has tested whether it multiplies on its own. If it does, it is worth more than shown.
  • The parry figures are player tests, not NCSOFT's: 6.7% for each 100 Accuracy short (tests run from 6.66 to 10) and half damage on a parried hit (20% to 50%). Read your own parry rate in Combat Analysis, in the BLOC column.
  • The sheets do not carry Accuracy, so the page starts from 1,000. Type your own.
  • Slots 1 and 5 count only Max Attack and Attack Bonus. PvE Attack, Boss Attack, Front Attack, Back Attack and Critical Attack are left out. All five are added after Attack Increase, so Weapon Damage Boost, the Power Shard and Attack Increase never multiply them, and each counts only under a condition: on a boss, from one side, on a crit, never in PvP. The one test on Global measured 10 Boss Attack at +0.13% against +0.27% for 10 Attack, less than the formula gives it, and nothing off a boss. Front, Back and Critical Attack are the bottom tier in Kanon's guide.
  • Max Attack and Attack Bonus sit within a tenth of each other on a Global sheet. Max Attack pulls ahead as your Weapon Damage Boost and Perfect grow, which is why Korean endgame guides rank it first.
  • You get three presets, each with its own lines on all five boards. Costs here are for the boards as you entered them.
