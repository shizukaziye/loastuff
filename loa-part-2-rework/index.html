The Part 2 Rework

Everything from the November 20, 2026 stream, in English
Korea only so far · no NA/EU date given

Smilegate used one stream to rebuild how a character grows. Gear drops instead of being crafted. Honing belongs to the slot, not the item. Three new slots arrive, six combat stats become four, and runes, the Evolution tree, the Ability Stone and Trade Skill tools all go. This page covers each change, then what your current gear turns into.

Balance

After the Nov 18 patch

Why the support gap grew

Players said the November 18 patch widened the gap between support classes when it was meant to close it. The team's answer:

  • The patch was one part of balance work built for Part 2. The whole set should have shipped together; they released the part that could go now.
  • They will narrow the gaps between classes.
  • In Part 2 the six combat stats become four, a new stat arrives, and they merge and redo every class's Specialization scaling.
  • Specialty Meter gain (the identity gauge) and damage from Specialization differ too much between classes today. They can't fix that inside the current system, so they are tuning it for Part 2.
  • Bard: the debuff that cuts a boss's Attack Power protects the whole raid today. It will change to cover only the Bard's own party. They said this will not turn Bard's one fixed seat in a raid into two, and they will tune the side effects with it.

A new balance target

  • Until now they balanced against all bosses together. From here on they balance against the newest raid.
  • That works because Part 2 lets more players into the newest raid, at different difficulties.
  • Where a raid favors different classes gate by gate, they want to fix that in the raid's design.
  • A balance patch will come before each new raid, and they will keep trimming until it is right.

For context, the November 18 patch added multi-crit, lengthened brand durations on all four supports, and made the attack buff from Bard's and Artist's ground zones last up to 6 seconds. Players on Inven's Bard board say Bard gained the most. Maxroll's summary of that patch has the numbers.

3

Combat Power comes first

Growth
  • Combat Power is the main measure of growth. Item level is shown first today; in Part 2 Combat Power is.
  • Combat Power sets your rewards. Entry to content needs both Combat Power and item level.
  • Engravings score the same. Today two classes at the same progress can show different Combat Power, and engravings are one cause. Every engraving a class really uses will score alike.
  • Supports score like DPS. A support and a DPS at the same stage of growth get the same Combat Power.
  • Inflated sources get trimmed. Paradise Orbs and gems add more Combat Power than real strength today. It can't match real DPS exactly, but they will correct the worst cases.

Three kinds of growth

New structure

Part 2 sorts every growth system by how long it should last.

Short term
Fast growth, big gains for the cost
Gear dropsGear refiningGear option change
  • The top players finish in 3 to 4 months.
  • Cheap, with large gains for what you spend.
  • The main path for new and old players alike: farm pieces and replace what you wear.
  • A finished set lasts about a year.
Mid term
Steady build-up, extended and eased once a year
Mana Infusion: PhaseEngraving slot upgradesHoning
  • Works like honing today: about once a year a new top opens and the range below gets easier.
  • They will add a safeguard against bad luck, like today's artisan pity.
Long term
Costs much time and gold, and holds its value
Mana Infusion: optionsGems
  • Anyone can raise these part of the way, but the return per gold is low.
  • This is where the top players compete.
  • No regular easing. The cap rises a little at a time and old progress keeps its worth.

More long-term systems exist but were not ready to show. They will arrive with the next raid and be explained at LOA ON on December 19.

One screen holds all of it, in six tabs: Refining, Honing, Mana Infusion, Ark Grid, Engravings and Roster Gems.

What changes

  • Three new slots: Belt 허리띠 on the left; Seal 인장 and Relic 성물 on the right.
  • Eight slots a side, sixteen in all. Accessories now carry an item level, and your item level is the average of all sixteen.
  • Special slots: Weapon, Vambrace, Seal and Relic cost more to raise and give more in return.
  • Your current bracelet's power moves to the Seal. You receive it as a Seal Scroll (see below). A Bracelet slot still sits with the accessories.

Alternating growth

The team called this the most important idea in Part 2 gearing.

  • The left side (armor, weapon, vambrace) gets its new tier in each summer update.
  • The right side (accessories, seal, relic) gets its new tier in each winter update.
  • So each side turns over once a year, and something new opens every six months.
  • The reason given: short cycles make a finished set short-lived, and long cycles leave players with nothing to do. The two sides were given the same number of slots on purpose.

Drops and refining

Short term

You no longer craft a set. You pick gear up, wear it, and feed copies into it.

Loot a pieceraids, Chaos Dungeon, Guardian Raid → Refine itfeed in the same piece, up to 5 times → Set its optionsAncient grade only

Drops

  • Raids are the main source, but Chaos Dungeons and Guardian Raids drop gear too.
  • Grades run from Normal to Ancient. Higher is stronger; Ancient is the goal.
  • The further you have grown, the better your odds of a higher-level drop. This holds for armor, weapons and accessories.

Refining 연마

  • Pick up the same piece for the same slot and you can refine with it.
  • No chance involved. Every attempt succeeds.
  • Five refinements a slot, each worth +5 item level.
  • Accessories refine the same way, on their own tab.
  • The team expects luck to matter less than with Ark Grid cores: any top player should reach the entry level of the next Nightmare raid. Drops are frequent, and rewards rise as you grow.

Option change 옵션 변환

  • Ancient gear only.
  • Each refinement opens one more option line.
  • The best result is known in advance, and a ceiling limits bad luck.
  • Spark 번뜩임: a glint to the left of a line. When it fires, that line takes its top value.
  • Meant to be cheap: play steadily and anyone ends up with top values.

7

Slot honing

Mid term · left side · yearly

How it works

  • You hone the slot, not the item. Swap the piece and the honing stays.
  • Honing raises the slot's power. It does not raise item level; grade and refinement do that.
  • Each step is worth about 3% at +21 and above, tapering to about 2% at +15 and below.
  • You must reach +25 before you can start the next tier.
  • When a new tier opens, the one below gets easier so that late starters can catch up. One idea, given as "probably": honing a lower tier with next-tier materials raises the success rate.
  • After each expansion they will set the odds a little hard early and easier late, so that players don't pile up at one level.

Materials and failure

  • Shards and leapstones go. So does Special Honing, the extra honing try you pay for with a raid leapstone alone.
  • Materials that Part 2 still uses carry over as they are.
  • The game swaps your shards, leapstones and Special Honing leapstones for tokens, which buy Part 2 materials. Rates follow market prices over the weeks before the change.
  • A token keeps the bind type of what it replaced: character-bound stays character-bound, tradable stays tradable.
  • Characters past a set level can turn character-bound tokens into roster-bound ones, as the honing exchange NPC does today.
  • The failure bonus is simpler. The example given: today a 0.5% chance climbs to 1% over ten failed tries; in Part 2 it starts at 1%.
Shown on stream · honing the Helmet slot

Helmet slot, +11 → +12. Success rate 5.00%, Artisan's Energy 6.97%.

  • Two materials only: 118 of one and 7 of the other, plus 22,567 silver and 1,565 gold.
  • An optional support material, 위대한 바늘 ("Great Needle"), 20 a try.
  • Buttons to hone once or three times in a row.
  • Slots listed: Weapon, Vambrace, Helmet, Shoulders, Chest, Belt, Pants, Gloves.

Mana Infusion

Mid to long term · right side · yearly

Mana Infusion (마력 주입) is one system in place of the game's several random-option systems. It covers the right side: accessories, Seal and Relic. It has two halves: rolling options, and raising the ones you keep.

Rolling options Long term

  • Pick a slot and pay materials and gold to infuse it.
  • Three options appear. Save the ones you like.
  • The screen shows what an option is worth in Combat Power, so you can compare without a calculator.
  • They will keep the reroll material rare.

Phases 위상 Mid term

  • Each infusion fills a gauge called Mana Saturation 마력 포화도.
  • A full gauge moves the slot to the next Phase, and the gauge keeps filling from there.
  • Seven Phases in all, extended once a year.
  • A higher Phase raises the numbers on the options you have saved.
Shown on stream · infusing the Relic slot

Relic slot, Phase 2, saturation 1,290 / 10,000. Combat Power 7,571.04 → 7,602.55. Cost: 10 of one material and 3,000 gold.

  • Max MP +25 (top grade)
  • Skill Power +0.24% (mid grade)
  • Base Attack Power +1,850 (high grade)
  • Reroll: 3 of 3 tries left, each costing one 마력 제어기 ("Mana Controller") and 750 gold.
  • Slots listed: Relic, Seal, Necklace, two Earrings, two Rings, Bracelet. Each slot has its own Phase.

Engraving upgrades

Mid term
  • Each of your five engraving slots can be raised to level 7.
  • No chance involved. You farm scrolls and spend them.
  • This takes over the job the Ability Stone did. Engraving recipes (books) stay as they are.
Shown on stream

Grudge, Cursed Doll, Raid Captain, Keen Blunt Weapon and Adrenaline, each slot at level 7, under the message "all engraving slots upgraded".

10.3Sidereal Weapons (Esther weapons)

  • A Sidereal Weapon becomes a piece you equip in the weapon slot. You are handed a new one, with an item level, and you can refine it.
  • Elgic levels work as tiers. (Elgic is the client's word for 엘라; many players say Ella.) Refining starts at Elgic 4 and raises both level and options.
  • You must finish all 5 refinements on Elgic 4 to move to Elgic 5, planned for summer 2027.
  • No separate honing. Refining a Sidereal Weapon raises the weapon slot's honing level with it.
  • The lead over normal gear: today an Evolution 10, Elgic 3 weapon sits 30 levels above a +25 weapon, worth 5 item levels on average. In Part 2 the lead is 40 levels, but spread over 16 slots that is 2.5.

10.4Bracelets become Seal Scrolls

  • You receive your Tier 4 bracelet as a Seal Scroll 인장 주문서. Use it and it sets your Seal slot to one of 21 grades.
  • Only your best one counts. The game scores it as a DPS bracelet and as a support bracelet and uses the higher result.
  • If you own only Relic bracelets, the game scores yours as if it were Ancient.
  • How the grade is set: every line (main stat, combat stat, single effects, paired effects) has a score for how strong it is, and the game adds the scores. Two paired effects need not score the same.
  • The plain attack and move speed line scores nothing.

10.5Accessories become scrolls

  • Only Tier 4 accessories with quality 67 or higher transfer.
  • Each transfers to the same slot and keeps its remaining trade count. Once you use it in the new system it binds to the character, as the November 6 stream said.
  • The result is a scroll of grade 1 to 6, worked out from auction prices and the odds of rolling the piece.
  • Market value counts for more than power. A three-high piece is only a little stronger than a two-high one but costs far more, and the transfer keeps that gap.
  • Prices after the stream do not count.
  • Even a grade 1 scroll does not set you at the top of the new system, but the grades keep their gaps in expected cost.
  • Dismantling an accessory gives Mana Infusion materials. Details will follow in a notice.

10.7Gems

  • Every gem becomes a Luminous Gem 광휘. The switch into Doomfire (damage) or Blazing (cooldown) form goes.
  • The option change for gems goes as well, because you now set gems on skills directly.
  • The October 29 stream said gems become roster-wide. The growth screen shown this time has a Roster Gems tab.
  • A new tier above level 10: Starlight Gems 성휘. Three level 10 gems make a Starlight level 1, which is what a level 11 gem would be under today's rule. From there up, two gems of one level make the next.
  • The cap is Starlight level 2 until the summer update.
  • You can trade gems, Luminous and Starlight alike.
  • Bound gems transfer the same way. A bound level 8 gem gives you a choice: keep it as a bound level 8, or take a level 7 transferred gem. The November 6 stream described that second option as a tradable level 7.

10.8Quality and Karma

  • Both go.
  • You get back what you put in as Mana Infusion items.
  • In setting the payout, the team weighed how freely these materials had been given out as rewards over the years.
  • Leftover Chaos Stones and Destiny Stones turn into the new materials too, as the November 6 stream said.

Combat rework

Ark Passive · stats · runes

Ark Passive, cut down

  • The Evolution, Enlightenment and Leap trees go. What remains is built around Enlightenment: choose a class engraving and its nodes switch on by themselves.
  • Why: Evolution nodes were meant to give players different ways to play, but everyone took the highest-damage set.
  • Damage that came from Evolution and Leap moves into passives and skills, holding today's power as closely as they can.
  • Leap's power moves into the tripods of Hyper Awakening Techniques.
  • Builds that stack Swiftness and rely on cooldown nodes won't feel the same after this alone. More changes are coming for them.

Four combat stats

  • Domination, Endurance and Expertise go.
  • Crit, Specialization and Swiftness stay, joined by a new stat: Prowess 기량.
  • Prowess gives a DPS more Outgoing Damage and a support more Ally Atk. Power Enhancement.
  • Stats leave the old trees, and you set them freely with points.
  • The game will set you up to fit your class engraving. Fine-tuning is up to you.
Shown on stream · the new Ark Passive window (Berserker)
  • Two rows, one for each class engraving: Berserker's Technique and Mayhem. Pick one and the other greys out.
  • Each row has six nodes that open at character levels 50, 52, 54, 56, 58 and 60.
  • A stat panel with 32 points to spend, 1 point a level, up to level 16 in each stat. The example put 16 in Crit and 16 in Specialization.

Economy

What Trust is for

Trust 신뢰도 is the Trust Point System that NA and EU have had since August 2025. You earn Trust by spending gold on growth, and you spend it when you send gold to another roster. Run short and you pay an extra fee of 25% of the shortfall.

Its aim is to protect the value of gold by charging accounts that sell gold without ever growing a character. The team said it has not worked as meant, because players get around it by handing over items in place of gold.

Part 2 rules

  • Any tradable item sent by direct trade costs the sender Trust, counted at the item's average market price.
  • Market sales cost no Trust.
  • Gems move to the market from the auction house.
  • The auction house closes for a time once Part 2 is live.
  • Accounts at zero Trust face limits. Keep sending and the score goes negative; send while negative and you must pay Pheons as a fee.

Trade Skills

Simplified

Trade Skills are what many players call life skills. The team's view: they are too hard for a new player, offer little fun, and yet gate materials everyone needs, such as fusion materials.

One skill, no tools

  • The team is cutting back skills, tools, materials and recipes alike.
  • The six Trade Skills share one level, which now runs to 100.
  • Trade Skill abilities and minigames get shorter.
  • Tools go. You get bound gold or the like for tools of Legendary grade or better.

Materials become Life Points

  • Everything you gather turns into Life Points 생활 포인트.
  • Life Points buy feasts, fusion materials and battle items.
  • Two kinds: roster-bound points tied to your stronghold, and tradable points.
  • The materials you hold now turn into Life Points as well.
