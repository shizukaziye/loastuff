The formula, with your numbers

This is the working model from Korean and Taiwanese player testing, mapped onto the Global client. NCSoft has never published one.

Attack( Pure Attack × (1 + Weapon Damage Boost) × Power Shard + Attack Bonus + base Attack ) × (1 + Attack Increase) + PvE, Boss and Back or Front Attack
Window( Pure Attack + Attack Bonus + base Attack ) × (1 + Attack Increase), which is the Attack your character window shows. Weapon Damage Boost and the Power Shard are not in it.
HitAttack × skill coefficient + skill flat damage − (Defense − Penetration) ÷ 10
Pool1 + Damage Boost + PvE Damage Boost + Boss Damage Boost − target Damage Tolerance
Crit1 + crit rate × (0.5 + Critical Damage Boost − target Critical Damage Tolerance)
Double1 + Double Chance − target Double Resist
Position1 + Back or Front Damage Boost × share of hits from that side
DamageHit × Pool × Crit × Double × Position × Multi-hit
DPSDamage × skill uses per second, which Combat Speed and cooldown reduction raise

Your buckets

BucketNowBuilt fromOne more stepGainStatus

  • Stats in one pool are interchangeable. Damage Boost, PvE Damage Boost and Boss Damage Boost add together, so 1% of any of them is worth the same against a boss.
  • A bucket with little in it pays the most. Double Chance and Back or Front Damage Boost each sit alone in their own multiplier, which is why they hold their value late.
  • Target tolerance comes out of the pool. Against 30% Damage Tolerance a small pool sits near 1.0, and every point of Damage Boost is then worth about 1%.
  • Flat adds dilute the multipliers before them. PvE Attack, Boss Attack and skill flat damage arrive after Weapon Damage Boost and Attack Increase, so those two only act on part of the hit.
  • Two characters with the same Attack in the window can hit differently. The window leaves Weapon Damage Boost out, and Weapon Damage Boost only multiplies the Attack that comes from gear, so where the number comes from matters.

Measure a target's Damage Tolerance

How sure each piece is

Everything here comes from player testing, client tooltips and datamined tables. Treat the tested rows as solid, and treat the rest as settings you can change.

Piece of the modelStatusWhat it rests on
Attack chain ordertestedPure Attack × Weapon Damage Boost, then Attack Bonus and base Attack, then Attack Increase, then flat PvE, Boss and positional Attack. Korean testers on Inven (March 2026), Taiwanese testers on Bahamut (December 2025) and kanonxo arrive at the same chain. In a Korean test, switching 5% Weapon Damage Boost on and off moved dummy damage by 2.8%, which puts gear at about 79% of that character's Attack.
Attack in the character windowtested in Korea and Taiwan(Pure Attack + Attack Bonus + base Attack) × (1 + Attack Increase), with the weapon at the middle of its range. Weapon Damage Boost is not in it. Players in both regions removed the Weapon Damage Boost nodes of the Marchutan board and the number did not move. In a Korean weapon swap it rose by 63, which is the 25.5 Attack difference × 1.212 closet × 2.014 Attack Increase. That character's Weapon Damage Boost, 69.4% with the main weapon on, would have pushed the change toward 105 if it were included. One Global reading does not fit yet: taking a 22 Attack helmet line off Bu lowered her Attack by 28, and the 17.1% Attack Increase visible on her gear and points predicts 26. An earlier version of this page read that as Weapon Damage Boost being inside the number. The check that settles it is to take the Revelation Amulet off: the Attack should not move.
Base Attackone readingKorean testers split the window into gear Attack and the Attack left with all gear off, which holds Attack Bonus plus Attack from level and class. Bu's gear, Attack Bonus and Attack Increase explain 2,444 of the 2,722 her window shows. The other 237, before Attack Increase, is entered as base Attack on every built-in sheet. Closet bonuses and collections would sit in the same gap. Measure yours with gear off.
One Damage Boost pool, minus target tolerancetestedTaiwanese tests from December 2025 and later Korean testing agree. A second tester's dummy and boss figures fit the same subtraction, at about 11% and 72% tolerance.
Crit multiplier: 1.5 plus Critical Damage BoosttestedAdditive on the 150% base in every test. Target Critical Damage Tolerance subtracts from it.
Crit rate capclient tooltip50% on Global, and Global boss parses stop there. Korea's client has stated 80% since July 1, 2026.
Critical Hit rating to crit rateplayer reports10 points per 1% on Global. Players report that Ludra needs about 1,700 Critical Hit for the 50% cap and that sheets near 1,200 see no crits there, which puts its Critical Hit Resist near 1,200. Korean testing fit 6.66% per 100, and the Korean reference sheet keeps that.
Double: twice the final hittestedResist is reported to come straight off your chance. Korean guides from Season 1, when Ludra was the top raid, count 1% Double as 1% damage in PvE, so the Ludra preset uses 0. Later Korean bosses carry 25% to 32%.
Back or Front Damage Boost as its own multipliertestedA 3.1% line measured as 3.099% in the Taiwanese tests.
Target tolerance and Defensemeasured below LudraAion 2 Maps read Global's bosses from fight logs: Damage Tolerance about 10% on the training dummy, 68% on tier 4 Transcendence and 58 to 67% on Conquest [Hard]; Defense 1,800, about 8,600 and 8,600 to 10,300. Those are the Global presets. Nobody has published Ludra's, so its preset takes 65% and 10,000 from the tier below it. Korean and Taiwanese figures differ: 44% and about 6,400 Defense on the last Transcendence stage-4 boss, 65.5% on a stage-10 boss. The solver above measures your own.
Power Shardpartly testedThe shard's 5% or 10% multiplies Pure Attack only, which is why a 5% shard tests at about 4.7% damage. The sheet's Power Shard Damage Bonus is counted as 100 points = 1% on top, following Grobs' calculator. The client tooltip only says it increases damage while a shard is on.
Perfectpartly testedThe tooltip says a Perfect hit deals enhanced damage based on the weapon's maximum Attack. The top of the range alone is worth half the range in Pure Attack. Korean tests on five classes in July 2026 put a Perfect hit 7% to 10% above an average one, more than the range gives, and another tester reports a hidden bonus of about 5%. The default adds 7% Pure Attack on Perfect hits, which comes to about 8% on a sheet like the one tested most thoroughly.
Multi-hitdisputedUp to four extra hits. Korea's aion2tool treats them as a chain: each extra hit rolls your chance again, and one to four extra hits add 11%, 25%, 43% and 67% in dungeons. At 38% chance that works out to 0.33% damage per 1% Multi-hit, the published Korean figure, and the Korean reference sheet uses it. Global is smaller: Aion 2 Maps measures 0.09 to 0.12% damage per 1% Multi-hit for one Ranger, with a 20% hidden base chance. The Global sheets keep one proc, worth 11% to match that. Both models are under Model choices.
Combat Speed and cooldownmodelCombat Speed shortens every cast, and the time it frees goes to filler skills. Cooldown reduction adds casts of cooldown skills and takes that time back. If fillers are worth a share ε of an average second, 1% faster casting is worth ε% and 1% more cooldown casts is worth (1 − ε)%. ε is the filler strength in the rail: filler share of damage ÷ filler share of cast time. The 50% default is an estimate from the Ranger's skill coefficients.
Penetration, Defense and flat skill damagerule tested, values fittedEach skill use loses 10% of Defense minus Penetration, and each skill adds flat damage of its own. Both shift what every Attack stat is worth against the Damage Boost pool. The defaults (skill multiplier 0.75, flat damage 1,000, boss Defense as above) are fitted to one Global Ranger's measured table: they put 100 Penetration at +0.17% and 10 Boss Attack at +0.13% on a boss, as measured. Other classes and rotations will differ; both numbers are under Skill and Defense.
Left outnot modelledAccuracy is a threshold: at or above the target's Block nothing is parried, a parried hit loses about half its damage, and back attacks skip the check. Proc passives such as Hunter's Soul, timed skill buffs, damage over time (which ignores Defense, crit and Double) and PvP are not modelled.
